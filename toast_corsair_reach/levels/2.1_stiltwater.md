## From the brief (the fuller picture: signature, mood, palette, beats, build notes)

World kinds and notes: Kinds for the world: **slicker** (0x78: yellow oilskins, dark straps), **marsh leech** (bug 0x122: slimy green-brown
spots), **storm midge** (flyer 0x2D: slate with electric-blue stripes), **bog walker** (undead 0x121: peat brown and
moss), **tower warden** (shielded 0x99: rain-dark steel), **bell lookout** (sniper 0x8C: grey hood), **cistern
crawler** (beetle 0xBD or 0xEF: pale and eyeless, white spots), **spire captain** (captain 0x12F: storm-blue coat
with a lightning stripe), **engine guard** (big shield 0x9C: brass and black).

**Rain and lightning**: see KIT.md (Studio writes the weather now; rain was seen in game).


- **Type**: outdoor, a branching boardwalk network over flood water (a different shape from every world 1 level).
  Footprint about 4,500 x 4,000. Floors: the marsh bed -40, the water's surface 0 (40 deep: every hero wades, no one
  swims), boardwalks 40-120, hut floors 120, hut roofs about 260 (sniper perches).
- **Signature**: **the leaning chapel**, a stone chapel sinking into the marsh at a slant, its nave walked through
  half under water.
- **Mood**: storm. Blue-grey ambient (0.25, 0.3, 0.35), a dim cool light from above (0.5, 0.55, 0.6), warm orange
  pools round every lantern post (the colony's lanterns still burn). A dark storm sky. Fog dark blue-grey and closer
  than world 1's (near 985, far 1019). A brooding tune.
- **Palette**: dark wet planks, mossy stone, reed and thatch roofs, mud, murky water (slow `texscroll`), rain-
  streaked plaster. Own images: the colony's emblem on signs, reed tufts, lit window panes.
- **Enemies**: slickers, marsh leeches, storm midges, bog walkers, 2 bell lookouts. Squads of 3-5; two squads fight
  together for the first time at the flood gate.
- **Weapon**: the machine gun chest again (on the landing jetty), for the world's switches.

Areas, in the order played:
1. **The landing jetty** (start, the south end). The heroes' ship tied up, a lantern post, the rain. A sign with
   the colony's emblem. The machine gun chest. Far to the north, the lighthouse on its rock with the storm engine's
   glow on top (a backdrop silhouette): the world's goal, lit by every lightning flash. Quiet.
2. **The reed beds** (about 1,200 x 900, open water with reeds). The boardwalk splits here: left to the huts, right
   across the reeds. 3 storm midges come down over the open water. From the junction the player sees the **flood
   gate** in the town wall to the north, its lock light on (the key is in the chapel).
3. **The hut cluster** (about 800 x 800, huts on stilts joined by boardwalks). 4 slickers among the huts (cover:
   hut walls, stacked crates, water barrels, modelled). **2 bell lookouts on hut roofs** (perches about 260), each
   with a lantern; one roof has stairs up (the player can climb and clear it, and a Tribal waits up there). A Tribal
   inside a hut. Health after.
4. **The mud islands** (mud banks rising out of the water, floors 10-40). 4 marsh leeches on the banks (they keep to
   dry floor), ammo and a medium health on the last bank.
5. **The leaning chapel** (a nave about 700 x 300, 500 high, tilted about 10 degrees, half flooded). 3 bog walkers
   among the pews (a turn towards dread: these are the drowned). The **flood gate key** on the altar dais at the far
   end, lit by candles. A Tribal in the bell loft up a stair.
6. **The fishers' rest** (a roofed platform with a brazier, on the way back to the gate): health, ammo, a Tribal.
   The quiet before the gate.
7. **The flood gate** (the town wall, about 600 wide, with the gate in it; floor 60). 4 slickers and 3 bog walkers
   (two squads) on a raised platform before it, with sandbags and carts for cover. The key door; behind it the exit
   into Bellmarket.

- **Gates**: one key door (the flood gate; key in the chapel).
- **Loop**: reed beds, huts, mud islands, chapel, back across to the reed beds.
- **Tribals (4)**: the climbable hut roof, the hut, the chapel's loft, the fishers' rest.
- **Secret**: under the biggest hut, wading between its stilts: a full health and a crate of ammo.
- **No traps**: every boardwalk edge drops into 40-deep water with ramps back up every 300 or so.
- **Story lines**: at the start "Mirefall. It hasn't stopped raining since the engine came."; at the huts "The
  colonists are hiding in their huts."; in the chapel "The chapel's going under with the rest of the town."; at
  the gate "The town's flood gate. The lighthouse is beyond the town."
- **Build notes**: the marsh bed as `terrain` (low, flat, the water in its hollow), boardwalks as `platform`s with
  ramps down into the water, huts as boxes on pilings, the chapel by hand (a tilted box nave, buttresses, a bell
  loft). About 3,500 triangles.

## As built

Built by `blender/stiltwater.py` on `common.py` (its own helpers: `deck_union` (one floor over overlapping deck
rectangles, plank sides only on the outline, none where a ramp or hut meets it, no floor under cover), `ramp`,
`mound` (a mud bank on the 100 grid), `gable`, `lantern`, `window`), the sky by `blender/sky_storm.py`
(`pack:sky/storm`, for 2.1, 2.2 and 2.4: storm clouds, no landmark), kinds `slicker`, `bell_lookout`, `storm_midge`,
`marsh_leech`, `bog_walker` in `blender/kinds.py`. **6,500 triangles, 264 KB unpacked** (plan about 3,500: the
marsh's water and its floor are 2,400 of them), 4 images of our own (the colony's emblem, reed stalks, a lit window,
sandbags), 12 game textures, polyList 152 (need 101). Fog (60, 70, 85) 985 / 1019, sky `pack:sky/storm`, rain
`{"like": 42, "weatherFlags": 1, "weatherIntensity": 180, "weatherOpacity": 220}` (flags 1: the level keeps its own
fog, so the Lua flashes and the dome's horizon match; intensity under 192: no lightning of the game's own), lightning
from `story.lua`, music `pack:music/stiltwater_outskirts`. **No game flag. Key: light blue** (keyLock 6), the flood
gate. Start points **0** (from 1.3) and **1** (for 2.2's way back). Tribals 4, snipers 2.

The water is 40 deep everywhere (floor -40, surface 0): every hero wades anywhere, so the boardwalks guide rather
than force the route. All footprints standing in the water sit on the 100 grid (x mostly on 200): `water_box` runs
its grid through every footprint's edge across the whole marsh. The water's vertex colours are set murky after the
bake. Banks over 60 degrees to 360 close the west, south and east (colliders behind their tops), the town wall (450,
a collider over it) the north.
