## From the brief (the fuller picture: signature, mood, palette, beats, build notes)

World kinds and notes: Kinds for the world: **furnace hand** (0x78: leather apron brown, soot), **ember beetle** (beetle 0xBE: the heat
pattern), **cinder wasp** (flyer 0x29: ash grey and orange), **forge turret** (turret 0xF5: soot black, glowing
seams), **slag gunner** (armoured gunner 0x143: black basalt with glowing orange seams; the game uses them mostly as
sentries), **catwalk lookout** (sniper 0x8C: soot and goggles), **forgemaster** (captain 0x12F: obsidian and gold),
**crown guard** (big shield 0x9C: molten gold), **vent swarmer** (0xE1: pale ash), **ash walker** (undead 0x121).

**Lava**, everywhere in the world: a surface over a solid floor 30 under it (the guide), banks under 40 high so a
hero who falls in walks straight out, `texscroll` on it, a red-orange glow baked into the vertices near it (brighten
the `Col` colours by distance to the lava), glows along it. Lava is never on the required path.


- **Type**: indoor, a multi-floor building: square rooms off right-angled corridors, a floor over a floor
  (`building`). Footprint about 3,500 x 3,000. Floors: the ground floor at 350 (carrying on from the flats), the
  gallery at 600, the catwalks at 850; rooms 700-900 high (the casting hall 900).
- **Signature**: **the casting hall**: a huge hall with a channel of molten metal down its middle, a gallery round
  it, catwalks across it; crossed first on the floor under fire from the catwalks, later on the catwalks above it.
- **Mood**: indoor heat: a dark ambient (0.12, 0.07, 0.06), strong orange from the molten channel and the furnaces
  (vertex pools), red warning lamps, smoke high in the hall (`fogzone`), machines (`audio`). The flats' tune or a
  harder one.
- **Palette**: brick (furnaces), iron plate (walls and floors), grating (catwalks: keep it opaque, see the guide on
  see-through surfaces), rust, molten metal (the lava texture, scrolled). Own images: hazard stripes, gauges, the
  sigil.
- **Enemies**: furnace hands, forge turrets, catwalk lookouts (new; 3 here), forgemasters (new), slag gunners. A
  patrol on the gallery. Squads of 4-6.

Areas, in the order played:
1. **The loading dock** (start, inside the forge gate): crates and ore carts, a crane, quiet.
2. **The furnace rooms** (three rooms off a right-angled corridor): furnace mouths glowing (glows), 3 furnace hands
   in each of two rooms, a Tribal in the third. Ammo.
3. **The casting hall floor** (about 1,400 x 900, 900 high). The molten channel down the middle, crossed by two
   narrow iron bridges. **2 forge turrets** on plinths, **3 catwalk lookouts** on the catwalks above (perches about
   500 over the floor) under red warning lamps, ingot stacks and moulds as cover. 3 forgemasters guard the stair up
   to the gallery.
4. **The gallery offices** (the gallery at 600, rooms off it). A patrol of 4 furnace hands walks the gallery (a
   chain). The gallery's far door is a switch door: shoot the crane's release across the hall. In the foreman's
   office: the **catwalk key** and a Tribal. The gallery squad keeps a door locked that opens back down to the
   loading dock: the shortcut.
5. **The upper catwalks** (850, 200 wide, railed with a parapet and a collider over the hall). Across the hall above
   where the player fought; the lookouts' perches reached and cleared; a view straight down into the channel. A key
   door (the catwalk key) to the bellows chamber.
6. **The bellows chamber** (about 900 x 700). The giant bellows (modelled) that blow the furnaces. 2 slag gunner
   sentries and 4 furnace hands. Their squad locks the exit door up to Caldera Crown. A side door, marked by a cold
   blue light, leads to the Coolant Vents.

- **Gates**: the gallery switch door, the catwalk key door, the squad-locked exit and shortcut doors.
- **Loop**: floor, gallery, back down to the dock; then up to the catwalks.
- **Tribals (4)**: a furnace room, the foreman's office, a catwalk alcove, behind the bellows.
- **Secret**: a crawlspace under the casting hall's moulds (a passage 150 high): a full health and ammo.
- **Story lines**: at the start "The Bellows. They pour the engines' hearts here."; at the hall "Snipers on the
  catwalks. Keep to the moulds."; at the exit "The Crown is up the mountain."
- **Build notes**: `building` with `from_floor` links up to the gallery and catwalks, the channel a hollow with the
  lava surface, catwalks as `platform`s, the bellows by hand. About 4,000 triangles.
