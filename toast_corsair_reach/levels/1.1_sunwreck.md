## From the brief (the fuller picture: signature, mood, palette, beats, build notes)

World kinds and notes: Kinds for the world (one or more new in each level): **corsair deckhand** (0x78: red-and-white striped shirt, black
bandana), **sand skitterer** (0xE1: sand-speckled), **gull raider** (flyer 0x54: white and grey, orange beak colour),
**lookout** (sniper 0x8C: navy coat, gold buttons), **boarder** (shielded 0x99: tan canvas, rope bands), **first
mate** (captain 0x12F: long red coat, gold trim), **cave crab** (beetle 0xEE or 0xBE: coral orange with barnacle
spots), **vault guard** (big shield 0x9C: black and gold), **drowned crew** (undead 0x121: seaweed green-grey).


- **Type**: outdoor, open ground round a feature, with a loop. Footprint about 4,000 x 3,000. Floors from -60 (the
  shallows' sand) to +420 (the gate), in four tiers: shallows, beach (0), dunes (60-140), cliff path (140-420).
- **Signature**: **the beached hull**, a corsair ship lying on its side across the beach, about 900 long and 260
  tall, walked through from a breach in the stern to a hole by the bow.
- **Mood**: noon. Sun high and warm white (about 1.0, 0.97, 0.9), sky-blue ambient (0.45, 0.55, 0.7). A clear day
  sky from one of the game's bright outdoor levels. The lightest haze of the pack (near 988, far 1021, pale blue).
  A bright, easy outdoor tune.
- **Palette**: bright sand (repeating, the main floor), darker wet sand at the shore (vertex colour, not a new
  texture), grey-brown rock for the cliffs and arches, grass on the dune tops, weathered planks and dark ribs for the
  hull, thatch for the huts, water. Own images: the corsairs' flag (a skull over crossed oars), a painted warning
  sign.
- **Enemies**: deckhands, sand skitterers, one squad of gull raiders, one lookout (the first sniper, an easy one).
  Squads of 2-3; the lookout with a wide `aimSpread` so its shots miss more.

Areas, in the order played:
1. **The sandbar** (about 600 x 400, floor 0, the level's south end). Start here, beside a broken rowboat, facing
   north along the beach. No enemy in view. Palms and a rock arch frame the view: the beach curving away, the hull
   on it, and high on the north-east headland the corsairs' gate with a red lock light, and their watchtower behind
   it (a backdrop silhouette beyond the rim). The player sees where they must go before they take a step.
2. **The burnt village** (about 900 x 700, floors 20-60, west of the beach). Five burnt fishing huts on low stilts,
   palms, overturned boats, nets on frames. First fight: 3 deckhands, a smart squad, with the huts' walls, the boats
   and big rocks as cover (one per member). One hut is whole: a Tribal inside. Crates with ammo, a small health after
   the fight.
3. **The crescent beach** (about 1,600 x 600, floor 0, along the water). Open sand with rocks every 400-600 for
   cover, tide pools, driftwood. The sea to the east: water in a hollow over sand sloping from 0 to -60, ending at a
   reef with surf (`texscroll`) and a collider behind it; beyond, a flat sea backdrop. Halfway along: 3 gull raiders
   drop in over the open sand. Medium health by the hull's stern after.
4. **The beached hull** (about 900 x 220, the floor tilted, rising from 0 to 120). In through the stern breach: the
   hold, lit through holes in the planks (`lightbeam`). 2 sand skitterers come at the player from the far end
   (ahead, never behind). A ramp of fallen cargo (steps of about 70) climbs to the deck rail, where the **gate key**
   lies in an open sea chest (`place_key_door`, the key here, the door at the gate). Off the hold: the captain's
   cabin with a Tribal. Out through the bow hole onto the dunes.
5. **The dunes** (about 1,000 x 800, floors 60-140, north-west). Rolling dunes, grass tufts (`nocollide`
   decoration), a corsair camp: 3 deckhands behind crates, a cookfire (glow) and two explosive drums beside them.
   Ammo after. A path back down to the burnt village closes the loop.
6. **The cliff path** (a canyon path 250 wide, winding up the headland from 140 to 420 over about 1,200, two
   switchbacks, slopes under 20 degrees). At the first switchback, **one lookout** on a rock spire across the gap,
   about 250 above the path, with a lantern beside it: the player sees it from below, and boulders on the path give
   cover between its shots. It can't be reached; shooting it is the lesson. Wind sound.
7. **The gate** (a landing about 300 x 300, floor 420). A wooden palisade gate between two rock pillars: the key
   door. Before it a lookout point over the harbour below (the next level, as a backdrop): the docks, the moored ship
   and, in the cliff beyond, torchlight. The ending beat: the gate opens on the view; the exit is behind it.

- **Gates**: one key door (the gate; key in the hull).
- **Loop**: village, beach, hull, dunes, back down to the village.
- **Tribals (3)**: the whole hut in the village, the hull's cabin, the tide-pool cave (secret).
- **Secret**: the tide-pool cave under the south cliff, reached by wading the shallows round the sandbar (under 40
  deep, so every hero walks): a Tribal, a medium health and, up a ledge (optional grab), an ammo crate.
- **Story lines**: at the start "Driftwood Cay. The corsairs came in on the morning tide."; in the village "The
  village is burnt. Its people were taken."; at the top of the hold "Their gate key. Careless."; at the lookout
  point "The harbour's theirs. Whatever they took, it's down there."
- **Build notes**: `terrain` (hills, low height) for the beach and dunes with `flat` spots for the sandbar, the
  village, the camp and the gate; the cliff path as a `canyon` link or `paths` with depth; the hull by hand in
  `bmesh` (a tilted hull shell, ribs as boxes, a plank floor, about 600 triangles); huts as boxes with thatch roofs.
  Aim for about 2,800 triangles.

## As built

Passed the owner's play-test after four rounds (JFG-Mods 6a93b33). The cliff path is three stacked legs (x -250 to 950 at z -1050, -1300, -1550),
  each a block whose wall is the next one's cliff, so it is longer than the brief's 1,200 (about 3,200 walked);
  the gate is at x 1250, z -1550, floor 420, the exit just behind it. The lookout's region covers the whole path
  (members only notice a hero inside a region). Grass tufts were dropped: decoration that isn't solid can't share a
  piece of the level with the sea. 2,882 triangles, 136 KB (over the 121 KB warning, under the buffer). Its gate's
  exit now leads into 1.2's start point 0; 1.2's way back arrives at 1.1's start point 1, by the gate (1080, 420,
  -1550), facing the gate.
