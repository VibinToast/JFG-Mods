## From the brief (the fuller picture: signature, mood, palette, beats, build notes)

World kinds and notes: Kinds for the world: **slicker** (0x78: yellow oilskins, dark straps), **marsh leech** (bug 0x122: slimy green-brown
spots), **storm midge** (flyer 0x2D: slate with electric-blue stripes), **bog walker** (undead 0x121: peat brown and
moss), **tower warden** (shielded 0x99: rain-dark steel), **bell lookout** (sniper 0x8C: grey hood), **cistern
crawler** (beetle 0xBD or 0xEF: pale and eyeless, white spots), **spire captain** (captain 0x12F: storm-blue coat
with a lightning stripe), **engine guard** (big shield 0x9C: brass and black).

**Rain and lightning**: see KIT.md (Studio writes the weather now; rain was seen in game).


- **Type**: mixed, a hub and spokes: the market square in the middle, a loop of streets round it, a bell tower
  climbed inside. Footprint about 3,800 x 3,800. Floors: the square's cobbles -25 under water 25 deep (a shallow
  basin), the streets 0, arcades 60, house upper floors 250, the bell chamber 700 (the tower about 900 tall).
- **Signature**: **the bell tower**, climbed inside on stairs round its walls, looked down from through its windows,
  with the first key in the bell chamber.
- **Mood**: the storm over a town: the outskirts' light and fog, more lanterns, lit windows (own images glowing),
  the tower lit from inside. Rain and lightning. The outskirts' tune or another of the same mood.
- **Palette**: cobbles (floor), plaster and timber houses, slate roofs, brick, dark wood, the bells' iron, water.
- **Enemies**: tower wardens (new), slickers on a patrol, bog walkers, 2 bell lookouts outside the tower and fights
  on its landings. Squads of 4-5.

Areas, in the order played:
1. **The gate arcade** (start, inside the flood gate, south side of the square). A covered arcade, quiet, and the
   view of the whole hub: the fountain in the middle; the bell tower to the north-west; the cistern's arched
   entrance to the east under a blue lamp; and to the north the **causeway gates**, two doors one behind the other in
   a short passage, each with its own lock light.
2. **The market square** (about 1,400 x 1,200, the shallow flooded basin, steps up to the arcades on every side).
   Market stalls (solid counters about 100 tall, canopies), the fountain (the landmark, and cover). 4 tower wardens
   and a squad of 4 slickers on a patrol ring round the fountain (`place_patrol`, `loop`; keep its links clear of the
   stalls). **2 bell lookouts on the tower's outer balconies** (perches about 450 and 600), lanterns beside them,
   covering the square: the player can shoot them from the arcades' cover, or later from inside the tower's windows.
3. **The street loop** (two streets 250 wide leaving the square west and east, joined behind the houses by a back
   lane to the north). The west street: 3 bog walkers, and an open house with a Tribal and a medium health. The loop
   reaches the tower's foot (west) and the cistern door (east).
4. **The bell tower** (indoor, about 600 x 600, 900 tall). Its door is a switch door: the pulley that holds it hangs
   over the door, seen from the street (shoot it). Inside, stairs round the walls climb four floors (0, 180, 360,
   540; stairs under 35 degrees, landings at each floor): 3 slickers on the first landing, 2 tower wardens on the
   third, a window on the second that looks out at the outer lookouts' balconies (shoot them from here if they're
   still up). The **bell chamber** at 700: the first causeway key and a Tribal, and from its door a rooftop walkway
   over the houses back down to the arcade by the causeway (drops under 200 onto roofs, then a stair): the shortcut.
5. **The cistern door** (east side, under the blue lamp): an arched door down to the exit into the Undercistern.
   The Undercistern's exit comes back to a start point of its own by the causeway gates (area 6), so the return is
   short.
6. **The causeway gates** (north). Two key doors in series: the outer opens with the tower's key, the inner with the
   cistern's (`place_key_door` with `key_level` the Undercistern). 3 tower wardens and 3 bog walkers hold the
   passage's mouth. Behind the inner gate, the exit to Stormspire.

- **The choice**: the tower and the cistern can be done in either order. Make both visible from the start, and give
  each its own feel: the tower's warm light up high, the cistern's cold blue below.
- **Gates**: the tower's switch door, two key doors (one key here, one carried back from the cistern).
- **Loop**: the square and the street loop; the rooftop shortcut from the tower.
- **Tribals (5)**: the west street house, the tower's second floor, the bell chamber, an arcade loft, the fountain's
  grotto (secret).
- **Secret**: the fountain's grotto: wade round the fountain to a gap in its base (40 deep at most): a Tribal and the
  world's **health upgrade**.
- **Story lines**: at the start "Bellmarket. The water's in the streets now."; at the causeway gates "Two locks. The
  colonists say one key's up the bell tower, and the other went down the cistern."; in the bell chamber "From up
  here you can see it: the engine on the lighthouse, pulling the clouds down."; when both gates are open "The
  causeway's clear."
- **Build notes**: `building` for the houses and the tower's rooms, the square as a shallow basin in a flat
  `terrain` or a modelled floor, stalls and fountain as blocks. About 4,000 triangles.

## As built

Built by `blender/bellmarket.py` on `common.py` (its own helpers: `ground` (one floor over the union of the streets'
rectangles on the 100 grid plus every edge, cells joined in pairs, none under cover), `house` (a solid house: walls
on the sides that face the street, a gable, lit windows; a gable end left out against a taller neighbour), `jambs`
(an opening's reveal), `slab_ramp` (a ramp slab in the tower), `gable`, `window`, `lantern` (from 2.1)), the storm sky
`pack:sky/storm` (2.1's), the new kind `tower_warden` in `blender/kinds.py`. **5,094 triangles, 207 KB unpacked**
(plan about 4,000: the streets' floor is 940 of them, the houses 1,218), 4 images of our own (a lit window, the
cistern's blue lamp, the market's green awning, the bell sign), 12 game textures, polyList need 86 (under the game's
90, so the build leaves it). Fog (60, 70, 85) 985 / 1019, rain as 2.1 (`{"like": 42, "weatherFlags": 1,
"weatherIntensity": 180, "weatherOpacity": 220}`), lightning from `story.lua` (2.1's row), music
`pack:music/drowned_bellmarket`. **Game flags: 106** (the tower pulley switch) **and 107** (the grotto's health
upgrade, `upgrade` 60). **Keys: lime** (keyLock 7, the outer causeway gate; the key in the bell chamber) and **blue**
(keyLock 11, the inner gate; its key is 2.3's to place, see 2.3's Links). Tribals 5, snipers 2.

The streets are at 0, the flooded square a basin (cobbles -25, water -5: 20 deep, every hero wades) with the fountain
court standing out of it (paving at 0, a step of 25 up from the water). Every floor and every cover block sits on the
100 or 50 grid, so the floor under each block is left out exactly with no slivers. The level is closed by solid
houses (eaves 340-420: none can be climbed), the tower, the causeway's walls (450) and a collider ring outside them.
