## From the brief (the fuller picture: signature, mood, palette, beats, build notes)

World kinds and notes: Kinds for the world: **slicker** (0x78: yellow oilskins, dark straps), **marsh leech** (bug 0x122: slimy green-brown
spots), **storm midge** (flyer 0x2D: slate with electric-blue stripes), **bog walker** (undead 0x121: peat brown and
moss), **tower warden** (shielded 0x99: rain-dark steel), **bell lookout** (sniper 0x8C: grey hood), **cistern
crawler** (beetle 0xBD or 0xEF: pale and eyeless, white spots), **spire captain** (captain 0x12F: storm-blue coat
with a lightning stripe), **engine guard** (big shield 0x9C: brass and black).

**Rain and lightning**: see KIT.md (Studio writes the weather now; rain was seen in game).


- **Type**: indoor, a corridor maze of square rooms round a great cistern hall, the key at its heart (`building`).
  Footprint about 3,000 x 3,000. Floors from 0 down to -300 in three tiers by stairs; vaults 600-700 high, corridors
  200 wide and 300 high.
- **Signature**: **the great cistern**, a vast pillared hall under knee-deep water, a forest of pillars for cover,
  the key on a plinth in the middle and a gallery round it above.
- **Mood**: cold and dark: deep blue ambient (0.07, 0.1, 0.16), a green tint from the water, sparse warm oil lamps,
  drips and running water (`audio`), mist in the great cistern (`fogzone`), the channels flowing (`texscroll`). A
  slow, dread tune.
- **Palette**: old brick (repeating walls), flagstones, slimy stone, iron (grates), water. Own images: iron grates,
  the colony's old water marks.
- **Enemies**: cistern crawlers (new), marsh leeches, small fast swarmers (0xE1, reskinned as **drain rats**, a
  second new kind if you like), bog walkers, one bell lookout. Squads of 4-5.

Areas, in the order played:
1. **The entry stair** (start, from the hub's cistern door): down to the first gallery.
2. **The pump gallery** (about 600 x 400): old pumps, 3 cistern crawlers. The way on is a sluice door held by a
   valve wheel seen through bars across a channel: shoot it (a switch door).
3. **The cell block** (a corridor with cells on both sides): **3 Tribals behind glass** in cells (square openings,
   `place_glass`, sills at most 40 over the floor). 4 marsh leeches in the corridor. Ammo.
4. **The channel walks** (walkways 150-200 wide beside water channels, a loop through two side tunnels): drain rats
   in packs of 3 out of side tunnels (seen coming), an ammo cache at a dead end, a Tribal at another.
5. **The guard room** (small, quiet, before the hall): health, ammo, a Tribal.
6. **The great cistern** (about 1,200 x 1,000, 700 high, floor -300, water 30 deep, pillars about 120 across every
   300). The **causeway key** on a plinth in the middle (`place_key_door`'s key for the hub's inner gate). 5 cistern
   crawlers among the pillars, 3 bog walkers rising from the far end, **a bell lookout on the gallery** (perch about
   250) with a lamp. The gallery can be reached by a stair for ammo and a view.
7. **The return stair** (the exit): up to a door, into the hub at the start point by the causeway gates.

- **Gates**: the sluice switch door.
- **Loop**: the channel walks back to the pump gallery.
- **Tribals (5)**: three cells, the channel dead end, the guard room.
- **Story lines**: at the start "The cistern. The whole town drank from here."; at the cells "Colonists, locked in.
  The corsairs made it a jail."; at the key "The causeway key. Back up to the square."
- **Build notes**: `building` rooms and right-angled corridors on three tiers; the great cistern a big room with
  pillars as boxes (one per crawler at least, for cover); channels as long shallow hollows. About 3,500 triangles.
