## From the brief (the fuller picture: signature, mood, palette, beats, build notes)

World kinds and notes: Kinds for the world: **furnace hand** (0x78: leather apron brown, soot), **ember beetle** (beetle 0xBE: the heat
pattern), **cinder wasp** (flyer 0x29: ash grey and orange), **forge turret** (turret 0xF5: soot black, glowing
seams), **slag gunner** (armoured gunner 0x143: black basalt with glowing orange seams; the game uses them mostly as
sentries), **catwalk lookout** (sniper 0x8C: soot and goggles), **forgemaster** (captain 0x12F: obsidian and gold),
**crown guard** (big shield 0x9C: molten gold), **vent swarmer** (0xE1: pale ash), **ash walker** (undead 0x121).

**Lava**, everywhere in the world: a surface over a solid floor 30 under it (the guide), banks under 40 high so a
hero who falls in walks straight out, `texscroll` on it, a red-orange glow baked into the vertices near it (brighten
the `Col` colours by distance to the lava), glows along it. Lava is never on the required path.


- **Type**: outdoor: a canyon run opening onto wide flats. Footprint about 5,000 x 3,500. Floors rising from 0 (the
  landing) to 350 (the forge gate) in three tiers: the world climbs from its first step.
- **Signature**: **the lava river crossings**: three rivers of lava, each 200-300 wide, crossed by railed stone
  bridges under turret fire.
- **Mood**: a red dusk under ash clouds: a dim, low, red sun (0.9, 0.35, 0.2), dark brown ambient, the lava's
  red-orange from below. A dark red sky. Fog dark red-brown (near 986, far 1020). A heavy, driving tune. The volcano
  fills the north with the summit forge glowing at its top (a backdrop cone beyond the rim, about 100 triangles).
- **Palette**: black-grey basalt, cooled lava crust, ash ground, lava, iron (rails, bridges' rails), dressed stone
  (bridges). Own images: the forge-masters' sigil on banners, hazard stripes.
- **Enemies**: furnace hands, ember beetles, cinder wasps, forge turrets, slag gunners (sentries). Squads of 4-6.
- **Weapon**: the machine gun chest again (in the canyon), for the world's switches.

Areas, in the order played:
1. **The landing ledge** (start, a shelf of cooled lava). The view up the valley: the flats, the rivers' glow, the
   forge gate on its rise and the volcano with the Crown burning on top. Quiet.
2. **The basalt canyon** (250-350 wide, about 1,200 long, walls about 400 high, winding). Basalt columns as cover.
   The machine gun chest in a niche. 4 furnace hands where the canyon widens; then 3 ember beetles. Ammo, health.
3. **The flats** (about 2,000 x 1,500, floors 100-200, slag heaps every 400-600). The three lava rivers across the
   way, each with one bridge. 3 cinder wasps over the open ground. **2 forge turrets** on the far banks covering the
   first two bridges (the player crosses under fire, from slag heap to slag heap). At the third bridge, **2 slag
   gunners as sentries** at its far end: big, slow, armoured.
4. **The ore yard** (about 800 x 600, floor 220). Rails, ore carts as cover, a yard office with a Tribal. 4 furnace
   hands and 3 ember beetles (two squads). The yard's gate up to the rise is a switch door: shoot the rail signal on
   its gantry.
5. **The forge gate** (about 500 x 400, floor 350). The foundry's great door between two slag gunner sentries, lit by
   braziers. The gunners' squad keeps it locked; a `cutcamera` shot when it opens: the door grinding open on the
   foundry's glow. The exit is inside.

- **Gates**: the yard switch door, the squad-locked forge gate.
- **Loop**: the flats: two ways across the second river (the bridge, and a longer way round its source), joining
  before the third.
- **Tribals (4)**: the canyon niche, a slag heap hollow, the yard office, the Juno cache.
- **Secrets**: **the Juno cache**, an island in a lava pool off the flats holding a Tribal and ammo, reached only by
  walking the lava (Juno; say so in the README). A ledge in the canyon wall with a full health.
- **Story lines**: at the start "Cinderhold. The storm engines start here."; at the flats "Their summit forge: the
  Crown. That's the way up."; when the gate opens "The foundry doors."
- **Build notes**: the canyon as a `canyon` link, the flats as `terrain` (`ridges`, low) with the rivers as hollows,
  bridges with `jfg64.bridge`, the volcano a low-poly backdrop cone outside the boundary. About 3,500 triangles.
