## From the brief (the fuller picture: signature, mood, palette, beats, build notes)

World kinds and notes: Kinds for the world: **furnace hand** (0x78: leather apron brown, soot), **ember beetle** (beetle 0xBE: the heat
pattern), **cinder wasp** (flyer 0x29: ash grey and orange), **forge turret** (turret 0xF5: soot black, glowing
seams), **slag gunner** (armoured gunner 0x143: black basalt with glowing orange seams; the game uses them mostly as
sentries), **catwalk lookout** (sniper 0x8C: soot and goggles), **forgemaster** (captain 0x12F: obsidian and gold),
**crown guard** (big shield 0x9C: molten gold), **vent swarmer** (0xE1: pale ash), **ash walker** (undead 0x121).

**Lava**, everywhere in the world: a surface over a solid floor 30 under it (the guide), banks under 40 high so a
hero who falls in walks straight out, `texscroll` on it, a red-orange glow baked into the vertices near it (brighten
the `Col` colours by distance to the lava), glows along it. Lava is never on the required path.


- **Type**: vertical and outdoor: switchbacks up the volcano's face, a walk round the inside of the crater rim, then
  an arena on the summit. Footprint about 4,000 x 4,000. Floors from 600 (the Bellows' roof door) to 1,400 (the
  arena), with the backdrop of the flats and the foundry far below.
- **Signature**: **the Crown**, an arena: a ring walkway round a molten pit, the forge machine on an island in the
  middle reached by two bridges, enemies in waves.
- **Mood**: night, the sky glowing red from below, ash in the air (if the game's snow weather reads as falling ash,
  try it and ask the owner), the lava lake's light from the caldera, the forge's huge glow, wind. After the last wave:
  the forge's glow dims (Lua fog) to a calm dark red.
- **Palette**: basalt, ash, iron, gold trim (own image), lava. Own images: the forge-masters' banners, glowing runes
  on the forge.
- **Enemies**: everything at once: furnace hands, ember beetles, cinder wasps, slag gunners, catwalk lookouts,
  forgemasters, crown guards (new). Squads of 5-6, the most health and the tightest aim of the pack.

Areas, in the order played:
1. **The roof door** (start, on a ledge of the cone). The view down: the flats, the rivers, the foundry's roofs.
2. **The switchbacks** (a path 250 wide zig-zagging up the cone's face in four legs from 600 to 1,000, slopes under
   20 degrees, the outer edge a rock lip about 150 high with a collider above). **2 catwalk lookouts on rock
   outcrops one leg up**, firing down, braziers beside them; cinder wasps sweeping the face; ember beetles on the
   landings; boulders as cover on every leg. A Tribal in an alcove off the second leg.
3. **The ash shelf** (about 900 x 600, floor 1,000). A camp of 4 crown guards round a brazier. After: health, ammo,
   a Tribal. The last rest before the top.
4. **The rim gate** (a passage carved through the rim at 1,100). A switch door held by a chain counterweight across
   a gap: shoot it.
5. **The rim walk** (a walled path along the inside of the crater rim from 1,100 to 1,300; the lava lake far below,
   never reachable: the wall and its collider keep the player on the path). 2 slag gunner sentries on rock perches
   along it; 3 cinder wasps; a Tribal in a rim alcove.
6. **The Crown** (an arena about 1,400 across at 1,400: a ring walkway 300 wide round a molten pit with a shallow
   lava floor and steps out, two bridges to the forge island). Three waves, spawned by trigger zones as the hero
   goes in (`trigger` 1; untested in a pack level: if it fails, fall back to all three at once, spread round the
   ring): first at the entrance, 5 furnace hands and 3 forgemasters; at the ring's halfway point, 2 slag gunners and
   4 cinder wasps; at the bridges, 5 crown guards and 3 forgemasters, whose squad locks the forge core's door.
   Health and ammo between the waves, round the ring.
7. **The forge core** (on the island): when the last wave dies the core's door opens: a Tribal, the closing lines,
   the forge's glow dims, `jfg.complete_planet("cinderhold")`, and the exit: a shuttle pad door, to the game's usual
   way back.

- **Gates**: the rim switch door, the squad-locked forge core.
- **Tribals (4)**: the switchback alcove, the ash shelf, the rim alcove, the forge core.
- **Story lines**: at the start "Caldera Crown. Everything they build, they build up there."; at the ash shelf "Last
  chance to rest."; entering the arena "The forge-masters. All of them."; at the end "The Crown's fires are out."
  then "Mirefall stays dry; the cay stays free. Corsair Reach is quiet."
- **Build notes**: the cone face as `terrain` (`ridges`) with the switchbacks cut as `paths`, rocks as cover, the
  rim walk a walled `platform` path, the arena a `layout` room with a `pit` (with a lava floor, not a kill floor),
  `jfg64.bridge` to the island. About 4,000 triangles.
