## From the brief (the fuller picture: signature, mood, palette, beats, build notes)

World kinds and notes: Kinds for the world: **slicker** (0x78: yellow oilskins, dark straps), **marsh leech** (bug 0x122: slimy green-brown
spots), **storm midge** (flyer 0x2D: slate with electric-blue stripes), **bog walker** (undead 0x121: peat brown and
moss), **tower warden** (shielded 0x99: rain-dark steel), **bell lookout** (sniper 0x8C: grey hood), **cistern
crawler** (beetle 0xBD or 0xEF: pale and eyeless, white spots), **spire captain** (captain 0x12F: storm-blue coat
with a lightning stripe), **engine guard** (big shield 0x9C: brass and black).

**Rain and lightning**: see KIT.md (Studio writes the weather now; rain was seen in game).


- **Type**: vertical: a causeway to a lighthouse on a rock, then a climb round and up through the tower. Footprint
  about 2,800 x 2,800. Floors from 0 (the causeway) to 1,200 (the lamp room): the tallest climb of the pack.
- **Signature**: **the spiral climb**: a gallery path round the outside of the tower in the storm, flyers circling
  out over the sea, snipers on the turn above.
- **Mood**: the storm at its worst: lightning twice as often, wind (`audio`), the sea's surf all round, fog closing
  in over the sea. The lamp room's glow huge and cold blue. After the engine dies: the fog fades lighter and warmer
  (`jfg.fog`), the lightning stops.
- **Palette**: whitewashed stone (the tower), dark rock (the islet), iron railings, brass, lamp glass, sea. Own
  images: the engine's warning plates, glowing glass panels.
- **Enemies**: storm midges, slickers, tower wardens, spire captains (new), engine guards (new), 2 bell lookouts.
  Squads of 4-5; the lamp room the hardest fight of the world.

Areas, in the order played:
1. **The causeway** (300 wide, about 1,200 long, over the sea, parapets 140 high with a collider above). Broken
   parapet blocks and a wrecked cart as cover. 2 squads of 3 storm midges come down over it in turn. The lighthouse
   grows ahead.
2. **The keeper's islet** (about 900 x 900, floors 40-120). The keeper's cottage (health, ammo, a Tribal), sheds.
   3 engine guards at the tower door; their squad keeps it locked.
3. **The inner stair** (inside the tower, a round room about 500 across, stairs round the wall from 0 to 400).
   4 slickers on the landings. At the top a door to the outer gallery, held by a pulley across the stairwell (a
   switch door: shoot it from the stair below).
4. **The outer gallery** (a path 220 wide spiralling round the outside from 400 to 900, a parapet about 150 high and
   a collider well above it, balconies every half turn as fighting landings). 3 storm midges circling out over the
   sea; **2 bell lookouts on balconies one turn up**, firing down, lanterns beside them; 3 tower wardens on a
   balcony; a Tribal on a broken balcony (optional ledge grab).
5. **The keeper's floors** (inside, 900 to 1,100): storerooms, the engine's cables running up the walls (own models
   or props). 4 spire captains and 3 engine guards: their squads lock the lamp room's door.
6. **The lamp room** (a round room about 700 across at 1,200, windows all round looking down on the whole world).
   The storm engine in the middle, the Stormglass glowing in it (modelled, with glows). A last squad of engine guards
   at the engine. When they die: the engine's glow goes out (Lua: the fog lightens, the lightning stops), messages,
   `jfg.complete_planet("mirefall")`, and the keeper's lift door opens: the exit into Cinderhold's entry level.

- **Gates**: the squad-locked tower door, the stairwell switch door, the squad-locked lamp room door.
- **Tribals (4)**: the cottage, the broken balcony, a storeroom, the lamp room.
- **No traps**: every outside path has its parapet and collider; nothing reaches the sea.
- **Story lines**: on the causeway "The lighthouse. The engine's on top."; on the gallery "Don't look down."; in
  the lamp room "The Stormglass, wired into their engine."; when it dies "The engine's dead. The rain is easing."
  and "Their maker's mark: Cinderhold."
- **Build notes**: the tower as a cylinder with a spiral `ramp` path round it (segments of 45 degrees under 20
  degrees of slope), balconies as `platform`s, the islet as `terrain`. Repeating stone textures. About 3,500
  triangles.

## As built

Built by `blender/stormspire.py` on `common.py` (its own helpers for a 16-sided tower: `P`/`V`/`L` (points round the
tower, its corners, a side's own frame), `ngon`/`wedge`/`clip`/`tile_flat` (a flat floor clipped to a convex region
in small cells, holes left out), `side_wall` (a side with openings, in pieces), `side_reveal`, `lbox` (a block on a
side), `ring_ramp` (one side of the gallery, rising, in 2 x 2 pieces), `reveal` (2.3's), `lantern`), the new kinds
`spire_captain` (0x12F) and `engine_guard` (0x9C) in `blender/kinds.py`. **5,449 triangles, 224 KB unpacked** (plan
about 3,500: the gallery with its rails and posts is 1,243, the sea 416, colliders 148), 5 images of our own (the
engine's warning plate, the Stormglass's glow, the lift cage's lattice, a lit window (2.1's painter), the gatehouse's
portcullis), 11 game textures (the import counts 10), polyList need 281 (the build sets 422; 1.1's 414 passed its
play-test). Fog (55, 65, 80) 984 / 1018, sky `pack:sky/storm`, music `pack:music/stormspire`, **the world's heaviest
rain**: `{"like": 42, "weatherFlags": 1, "weatherIntensity": 191, "weatherOpacity": 240, "weatherVelX": -60,
"weatherVelY": -420, "weatherVelZ": 170}` (191: one under the game's own lightning, which Lua couldn't stop). Bake:
sun (0.45, 0.5, 0.6) high from the south-west, ambient (0.2, 0.25, 0.32); inside the tower 0.8 of that and warm
lamps; the lamp room tinted cold blue round the engine. **Game flags: 109** (the stairwell pulley) and **127** (set
by `story.lua` when the engine dies: the lightning stops for good and the warm fog comes back on later visits; no
object holds it). **No key.** Tribals 4, snipers 2.
