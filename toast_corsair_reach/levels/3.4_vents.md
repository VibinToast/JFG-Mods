## From the brief (the fuller picture: signature, mood, palette, beats, build notes)

World kinds and notes: Kinds for the world: **furnace hand** (0x78: leather apron brown, soot), **ember beetle** (beetle 0xBE: the heat
pattern), **cinder wasp** (flyer 0x29: ash grey and orange), **forge turret** (turret 0xF5: soot black, glowing
seams), **slag gunner** (armoured gunner 0x143: black basalt with glowing orange seams; the game uses them mostly as
sentries), **catwalk lookout** (sniper 0x8C: soot and goggles), **forgemaster** (captain 0x12F: obsidian and gold),
**crown guard** (big shield 0x9C: molten gold), **vent swarmer** (0xE1: pale ash), **ash walker** (undead 0x121).

**Lava**, everywhere in the world: a surface over a solid floor 30 under it (the guide), banks under 40 high so a
hero who falls in walks straight out, `texscroll` on it, a red-orange glow baked into the vertices near it (brighten
the `Col` colours by distance to the lava), glows along it. Lava is never on the required path.


- **Type**: indoor, a cluster of small round rooms packed together (a shape no other level has). Footprint about
  2,500 x 2,500, floors 350-500.
- **Signature**: **the steam cluster**: eight round vent chambers (radius 150-300) joined by short pipes (tunnels
  200 wide), filled with steam.
- **Mood**: the one cool place in the world: white-grey steam (`fogzone`), pale blue coolant glows against the reds,
  hissing and pumping (`audio`).
- **Palette**: iron plate, pipes, tiles, coolant (a scrolled blue surface over a floor, like water). Own images:
  gauges, valve wheels, hazard stripes.
- **Enemies**: vent swarmers (new) in packs of 4-5, ash walkers (new) in the dead ends. Squads of 4-6.

Areas, in the order played:
1. **The vent hatch** (start, from the Bellows' side door).
2. **The chamber cluster** (eight chambers in a loop with two cross-links): swarmers come in packs out of the pipes
   (seen and heard first), ash walkers wait in two dead-end chambers with a Tribal each. Ammo in most chambers.
3. **The pump hall** (about 700 x 500): the world's **health upgrade** and a chest with an optional weapon for the
   finale (rockets, weapon 5), 2 Tribals. A last fight first: 5 ash walkers and a pack of swarmers.
4. **The service lift** (the exit): a door into a passage that comes out at Caldera Crown's start (an exit into a
   start point of its own there), so the detour costs no trip back.

- **Tribals (4)**: two dead ends, two in the pump hall.
- **Story lines**: at the start "Coolant vents. At least it's cool."; in the pump hall "Their spare parts. Ours now."
- **Build notes**: `layout` with many small `round` rooms and short `tunnel` links. About 2,500 triangles.
