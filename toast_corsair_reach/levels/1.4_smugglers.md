## From the brief (the fuller picture: signature, mood, palette, beats, build notes)

World kinds and notes: Kinds for the world (one or more new in each level): **corsair deckhand** (0x78: red-and-white striped shirt, black
bandana), **sand skitterer** (0xE1: sand-speckled), **gull raider** (flyer 0x54: white and grey, orange beak colour),
**lookout** (sniper 0x8C: navy coat, gold buttons), **boarder** (shielded 0x99: tan canvas, rope bands), **first
mate** (captain 0x12F: long red coat, gold trim), **cave crab** (beetle 0xEE or 0xBE: coral orange with barnacle
spots), **vault guard** (big shield 0x9C: black and gold), **drowned crew** (undead 0x121: seaweed green-grey).


- **Type**: indoor, a winding tunnel network opening into small rooms. Footprint about 3,000 x 2,000. Floors from
  20 (the docks' side door) down to -150 (below the sea), tunnels 200 wide and 280 high.
- **Signature**: **the drowned tavern**, a sunken smugglers' tavern the tunnels pass through, its floor under
  shallow water, tables and a bar counter for cover, and its old crew still in it.
- **Mood**: night underground: dim green-grey ambient, candle glows, mist (`fogzone`) in the tavern, drips
  (`audio`). The grotto's tune or a darker one.
- **Palette**: old brick and stone, timber, barrels, water. Own images: the tavern sign, bottle shelves.
- **Enemies**: drowned crew (new), deckhands, one lookout. Squads of 3-4.

Areas, in the order played:
1. **The cellar stair** (start, from the docks' side door): a stone stair down into the dark.
2. **The rum cellar** (about 500 x 400): barrels (`damagecrate`, some explosive), 3 drowned crew, green light.
3. **The smugglers' tunnels** (winding, two branches making a small loop): a Tribal at a dead end, an ammo cache.
4. **The drowned tavern** (about 800 x 600, 650 high, floor -80, water 30 deep over most of it): 4 drowned crew
   among tables and the bar; **a lookout on the gallery** (perch about 180, on a balcony up a stair, with a Tribal
   behind it once the player climbs up).
5. **The strongroom**: seen first through a square window with glass (`place_glass`, its sill at most 40 over the
   floor): a chest and the **health upgrade** inside. Break the glass and climb in. The chest's weapon (optional,
   the shotgun, weapon 3) is the reward for the detour. 2 more Tribals in the strongroom's back.
6. **The grotto passage** (the exit): a door into a passage that comes out at the grotto's east landing (an exit
   into that level's start point there), past the grotto's first half.

- **Tribals (4)**: the tunnel dead end, the tavern gallery, two in the strongroom.
- **Story lines**: at the start "Smugglers' tunnels. Something's still down here."; in the tavern "The crew never
  left."; at the exit "Torchlight ahead: the grotto."
- **Build notes**: `layout` tunnels with `wiggle` and small `round` rooms; the tavern a `hall` room with tables and
  the bar as blocks. About 2,500 triangles.
