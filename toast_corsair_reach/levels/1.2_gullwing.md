## From the brief (the fuller picture: signature, mood, palette, beats, build notes)

World kinds and notes: Kinds for the world (one or more new in each level): **corsair deckhand** (0x78: red-and-white striped shirt, black
bandana), **sand skitterer** (0xE1: sand-speckled), **gull raider** (flyer 0x54: white and grey, orange beak colour),
**lookout** (sniper 0x8C: navy coat, gold buttons), **boarder** (shielded 0x99: tan canvas, rope bands), **first
mate** (captain 0x12F: long red coat, gold trim), **cave crab** (beetle 0xEE or 0xBE: coral orange with barnacle
spots), **vault guard** (big shield 0x9C: black and gold), **drowned crew** (undead 0x121: seaweed green-grey).


- **Type**: mixed: outdoor piers and quays, an indoor warehouse, a ship. A loop round the inner harbour. Footprint
  about 4,500 x 3,000. Floors: water's floor -40, water surface -10, quays and piers 20, ship's deck 90, the
  warehouse loft 220, the watchtower's balcony 260; the start is at 420 and comes down.
- **Signature**: **the moored corsair ship**, about 800 long and 200 wide, deck at 90, two masts with crow's nests
  at about 400. The deck battle under the masts.
- **Mood**: golden hour. Low sun from the west, orange (1.0, 0.75, 0.45), purple-blue ambient (0.35, 0.3, 0.5). A
  sunset sky. Warm haze (near 986, far 1020, orange-pink). Lanterns lit everywhere: warm pools baked into the floor
  round each. A tune with more drive than the beach's.
- **Palette**: planks (repeating, piers and decks), dark wet wood (pilings), stone quay blocks, plaster and brick
  (warehouse, gatehouses), roof tiles, water. Own images: the patched sail with the corsair emblem, rope coils, the
  painted market signs, a "keep out" sign on the side door.
- **Enemies**: deckhands, boarders, lookouts (4 snipers in this level), a first mate squad at the end. Squads of 3-4.
  One patrol.
- **Weapon**: the machine gun, in a chest in the fish market, on the required path. The level's switch comes after.

Areas, in the order played:
1. **The headland stair** (start, floor 420). Wide switchback ramps down the headland to the quay at 20. The whole
   harbour below at golden hour: the fish market, the canal with its gatehouse, the watchtower, the warehouse, the
   ship, and at the far end the harbour gate with its lock light. Quiet.
2. **The fish market** (about 800 x 600, floor 20). Stalls with canopies, nets, barrels, a crane. The **machine gun
   chest** at the back of a stall, facing the way in. Then the first fight with it: 3 deckhands and 2 boarders (two
   squads) behind the stalls (model the stalls' counters as solid blocks about 100 tall). A patrol of deckhands walks
   the promenade beyond (`place_patrol`, a chain along the quay). Health and ammo after.
3. **The canal gatehouse** (the first switch). A canal 350 wide cuts the waterfront; a stone bridge crosses it into
   a gatehouse with a door. The door is held by a **winch** on a post across the canal, lit by a lamp (the switch,
   facing the bridge): shoot it, the gatehouse door opens. A line of story points at it the first time.
4. **The watchtower yard** (about 700 x 700, tiers at 20, 70 and 120). Cargo stacks as cover, 3 boarders. The
   watchtower (about 600 tall) has a **lookout on its top** (perch about 500) covering the yard, lantern beside it,
   and stairs inside to a balcony at 260 with a Tribal (the top stays out of reach).
5. **The warehouse** (indoor, about 1,000 x 700, 650 high, floor 20, a loft at 220 up a stair). Crate stacks, a
   hanging crane hook, lanterns. 4 deckhands on the floor, **a lookout on the loft** at the far end. In the loft: the
   **ship key** (for the gangplank gate) and a Tribal in the corner. The warehouse squad keeps its back door locked
   (`place_squad` `door`): once they're dead it opens straight back onto the fish market, the shortcut.
6. **The ship** (deck at 90). A gangplank gate on the quay (the key door) and a ramp up. On deck: 4 deckhands using
   the hatches, cannons and cargo (blocks about 100 tall) as cover, and **two lookouts in the crow's nests** with
   lanterns hung beside them (they can be shot from the quay before boarding: let the quay's line of sight reach
   them). The stern castle's cabin: a Tribal and an ammo chest. From the bow, a plank to the far pier.
7. **The harbour gate** (about 600 x 500, floor 20). A fortified gate between two towers. 2 first mates and 3
   deckhands; the first mates' squad keeps the gate locked, with a lock light on its frame, and a `cutcamera` shot
   when it opens: the gate swinging open on the cliff and its torch-lit cave mouth. The exit is in the cave mouth.
8. **The smugglers' door** (the branch, off the fish market's back alley). A small door in a passage with a blue
   lantern over it and the "keep out" sign: the exit to Smugglers' Run. It looks like a side path on purpose.

- **Gates**: the canal switch, the ship key door, the squad-locked harbour gate, the squad-locked warehouse back door.
- **Loop**: market, gatehouse, yard, warehouse, back door to the market.
- **Tribals (4)**: the watchtower balcony, the warehouse loft, the ship's cabin, under the market pier (secret).
- **Secrets**: wading under the market pier (shallow water under the planks, with steps back up) to a smugglers'
  cache with a Tribal and ammo; a ledge onto a warehouse roof with a medium health.
- **No traps**: falling off a pier lands in water under 40 deep; give the shallows a way back up (steps or a ramp)
  every 300 or so.
- **Story lines**: at the start "The corsairs' harbour. The sun's going down; so are they."; at the winch "That
  winch holds the gatehouse door."; on the ship's deck "Their flagship. The hold's empty: the plunder's somewhere
  else."; when the harbour gate opens "Torches in the cliff. That's where they keep it."
- **Build notes**: piers as plank decks on six-sided pilings, the ship's hull by hand, the warehouse with
  `building`; repeating plank textures keep the cuts down. This one is heavy: aim for about 4,000 triangles, and cut
  pilings before cutting the ship.

**Closed:** the west and east halves are split by walls the hero can't climb (the dividing wall 320, the sea walls
300, the fort wall 450, the sheds' eaves 450, the warehouse's 600); every wall top within a jump is 250 or more over
the floor beside it; the ship and causeway have colliders over their rails. Boundary colliders behind the cliffs and
buildings only (never over open water). Water everywhere 30 deep, with steps out.

**Difficulty:** deckhands and boarders `aimSpread` 52 (1.1: 60), lookouts 75 (1.1: 90), smart deckhand squads
`flags` 729 (0x40 set). Drops: small and medium health. Snipers: 4 (tower, loft, two nests).
