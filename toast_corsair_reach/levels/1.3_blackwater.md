## From the brief (the fuller picture: signature, mood, palette, beats, build notes)

World kinds and notes: Kinds for the world (one or more new in each level): **corsair deckhand** (0x78: red-and-white striped shirt, black
bandana), **sand skitterer** (0xE1: sand-speckled), **gull raider** (flyer 0x54: white and grey, orange beak colour),
**lookout** (sniper 0x8C: navy coat, gold buttons), **boarder** (shielded 0x99: tan canvas, rope bands), **first
mate** (captain 0x12F: long red coat, gold trim), **cave crab** (beetle 0xEE or 0xBE: coral orange with barnacle
spots), **vault guard** (big shield 0x9C: black and gold), **drowned crew** (undead 0x121: seaweed green-grey).


- **Type**: indoor, a cave basin walked down round its sides (a tiered basin, descending). Footprint about
  3,500 x 3,000. Floors from 400 (the entry tunnel) down to -100 (the vault). The main cavern's roof about 1,000 to
  1,200 high (a set piece: taller than the game's usual 600-800), with a crack in it letting in moonlight.
- **Signature**: **the galleon in the cave**: a wrecked galleon lodged in the lagoon, half sunk and tilted, seen
  first from the high walkway and reached last; the way to the vault goes through its hull.
- **Mood**: night. Very dark teal ambient (0.08, 0.14, 0.18); a pale blue moon shaft through the roof crack onto
  the galleon (`lightbeam`, and the bake brighter there); warm orange pools round every torch (baked, and `light`
  objects); the lagoon faintly glowing teal (vertex colours, slow `texscroll`); low mist on the water (`fogzone`). A
  night sky for the crack. A tense, quiet tune.
- **Palette**: wet dark rock (repeating walls), cave floor rock, planks (walkways), dark galleon wood, water. Own
  images: rope, the corsairs' painted marks on the rock, gold coins and goblets for the hoard, the trade chart.
- **Enemies**: cave crabs, vault guards, deckhands and first mates again, 2 lookouts. Squads of 3-4.

Areas, in the order played:
1. **The torch tunnel** (250 wide, 300 high, about 600 long, floor 400 falling to 360). Lanterns, the corsairs'
   marks on the walls. Start here; quiet.
2. **The high walkway** (a wooden walkway along the cavern wall at 360, 220 wide, a solid rail about 150 high with
   a collider above it). The first view: the galleon below in the moon shaft, torches round the lagoon, and beyond
   the galleon the vault door with **two lock lights** (a key and a switch: see 6 and 7). Lock before key.
3. **The crab ledges** (rock shelves down the west wall from 260 to 160, joined by ramps). 4 cave crabs among
   stalagmites about 110 tall (cover, modelled). **A lookout on a rock pillar** across the water (perch about 320),
   lantern beside it. A Tribal in an alcove off a shelf.
4. **The rope bridges and the island** (two railed bridges at 160 from the west wall to a rock island in the
   middle, `jfg64.bridge`). On the island a corsair camp: 2 vault guards and 3 deckhands, tents and explosive drums.
   The **vault key** is up the island's lookout stair. A Tribal in a tent. A second bridge goes on to the east side.
5. **The east landing** (about 600 x 500, floor 40, a cave chamber with a jetty). Health and ammo. A stair climbs
   from here back up to the high walkway (the loop). Smugglers' Run's exit arrives here (a start point of its own).
6. **The galleon's gangway** (the switch). From the landing, the galleon's side: its hull door is held by a winch
   on the galleon's deck, lit, visible across the water. Shoot it and the hull door opens.
7. **Inside the galleon** (the hold as a corridor, tilted under 15 degrees, floors 0-80). Lantern-lit, cramped:
   3 deckhands and 2 first mates. **A lookout on the broken mast top** outside, covering the landing (seen from the
   landing before the switch). The captain's cabin with a Tribal. Out of the stern, a stair down to the vault door.
8. **The vault** (indoor, about 700 x 600, floor -100, behind the key door). The hardest fight of the world: 3 first
   mates and 2 vault guards among the hoard's piles. Then the treasure: a chest (an optional weapon: grenades,
   weapon 8), a full health, a lot of ammo, gold piled everywhere (own images), and **3 Tribals held in cells behind
   glass** (square openings, `place_glass`, their sills at most 40 over the floor so every hero climbs
   in). On a table, the corsairs' trade chart (own image).
9. **The sea door** (the exit). A tunnel from the vault to a hidden cove at dawn, where the heroes' ship waits: the
   exit into Mirefall's entry level.

- **Gates**: the switch (the galleon's winch), the key door (the vault). The world's planet completes when the
  player reaches the treasure.
- **Loop**: walkway, ledges, island, landing, stair back up to the walkway.
- **Tribals (6)**: the ledge alcove, the island tent, the galleon's cabin, the three vault cells.
- **Secret**: a ledge (optional grab) behind the waterfall at the lagoon's north end: ammo and a medium health.
- **No traps**: the lagoon is under 40 deep, its floor joins the landing, and every walkway is railed.
- **Story lines**: at the start "The corsairs' grotto. Mind the drop."; at the first view "A whole galleon, wedged
  in the rock. The vault must be past it."; when the hull door opens "The winch dropped the gangway."; in the
  vault "Their hoard... but the Stormglass's crate is empty."; at the chart "A trade chart: sold to the
  storm-builders of Mirefall."; when the planet completes "Driftwood Cay is free. Mirefall appears on the map."
- **Build notes**: the cavern and chambers with `layout` (`cave` rooms with `roof`, `tunnel` links); the walkways
  as `platform`s with rails; the galleon by hand (a tilted hull, a deck, a broken mast, about 800 triangles). Aim for
  about 4,000 triangles.
